# Cala 1.1.5

- Right-clicking (or Control-clicking) a tray icon no longer opens its panel: it shows a menu to move it to More, hide it, or bring it back to the bar.
- Press and drag a tray icon to reorder it, just like app icons.
- An icon that bounced for attention (like Slack) no longer stays higher than the others once it stops bouncing.
