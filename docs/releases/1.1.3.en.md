# Cala 1.1.3

- Mail actions are instant. Delete, archive, mark as read and the new "mark as unread" update the list as soon as you click. Cala syncs with the server in the background. If something fails or you are offline, the message comes back and a notice tells you what happened. Nothing is lost.
- Opening mail is instant. Recent messages are preloaded, and messages you have already seen open from a local copy on your Mac. New ones show the sender and subject right away.
- Cala reconnects quickly after your Mac sleeps or the network changes, instead of hanging for tens of seconds.
- Attachments appear inside the message. Click to download, preview with Quick Look or save to Downloads.
- Select several messages by clicking their avatar circle, then archive, delete or mark them all at once.
- Right-click a message in the list for quick actions.
- Scroll to the end of the inbox to load older messages.
