# Cala 1.1.8

- Usage forecasts now say how much will be left at reset, in the same words as the bars. For example "~15% left at reset · quick estimate". When the estimate is almost nothing, it says "High use" instead of "~0%".
- Clearer layout on the usage cards: the estimate stands out, warnings keep easy-to-read text with a small orange or red symbol, and the learning note is shorter and lighter.
- The reset time follows your 12-hour or 24-hour clock setting.
