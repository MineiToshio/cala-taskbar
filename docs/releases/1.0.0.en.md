# Cala 1.0.0

This is the first public release of Cala, a taskbar for your Mac. Thank you for trying it.

## What's in it

**The bar**
- Your apps at the bottom: click to switch, hover for up to six window previews per app, right-click to pin, open a new window or quit.
- A tray on the right with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sound, brightness, battery, disk, keyboard, Downloads and Trash.
- A clock with your upcoming events and a button to join the video call.

**Windows**
- Option + Tab shows up to 20 windows, minimized ones included. Type to search them.
- Zones: drag a window, right-click or hold Shift, and drop it into halves, thirds, a grid or a layout you draw. Each monitor can have its own layout.

**Insight and integrations**
- A CPU and memory panel that tells you which app weighs the most and when a restart is worth it.
- Mail in the tray: unread count for Gmail, iCloud and Yahoo, with read, archive and delete.
- Claude and Codex usage and quota alerts.

**Your choice**
- Switch each module on or off separately. Zones and the window switcher work without the bar.
- English and Spanish, following your Mac by default.
- Free, source available, no Cala account, macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

## Updates

Cala now updates itself. It checks once a day, and you can install a new version with one click from Settings › Help and diagnostics. You can also turn on automatic installation while your Mac is idle. Every update is signed and verified before it is installed, and Cala goes back to the previous version if a new one fails to start.

## First install

macOS asks you to allow Cala the first time you open it. The steps are explained in the [README](https://github.com/MineiToshio/cala-taskbar#readme).
