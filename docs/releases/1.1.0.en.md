# Cala 1.1.0

Two improvements to make the bar calmer and more yours.

- **App alerts in Cala's bar.** The native Dock no longer pops up when an app asks for your attention (for example, a notification). You now see it in the bar: the app's icon hops, or shows a steady ring if "Reduce motion" is on. If the bar is hidden, a soft glow appears at the bottom edge without opening the bar.
- While Cala runs, icons in the hidden native Dock don't bounce. Your previous Dock bouncing setting comes back when you stop hiding the Dock, quit Cala or uninstall it.
- **More precise opening with "Hide bar automatically".** The bar no longer opens when the pointer just brushes the bottom edge. It opens when you rest the pointer at the edge for a moment (about a quarter of a second) or push against it. Passing along the edge quickly, clicking a button at the bottom of a window, or holding the mouse button doesn't open it. If it opens and you move away without using it, it hides right away.
- Mail records internal timing measurements in your Mac's local debug log, to make it faster later. Nothing is sent anywhere and Mail works exactly as before.
